# Worksheets & Printables

Downloadable and printable worksheets for the Game Design & Programming Club. These can be used in class or at home for extra practice.

## Design Worksheets

- [Pixel Art Grid](/docs/worksheets/pixel-art-grid) -- Design 8x8 and 16x16 sprites on classic pixel art grids (just like designing characters on the Commodore 64!)
- [Level Design Planner](/docs/worksheets/level-design) -- Map out your 2D and 3D levels before building them
- [Game Design Document](/docs/worksheets/game-design-doc) -- Plan your game before you build it

## Practice Quizzes

Each week includes a practice quiz to test your knowledge:

- [Week 1 Quiz](/docs/curriculum/week-1/quiz) -- Game Design Foundations
- [Week 2 Quiz](/docs/curriculum/week-2/quiz) -- Sprites, Movement & World Building
- [Week 3 Quiz](/docs/curriculum/week-3/quiz) -- Enemies, Hazards & Level Design
- [Week 4 Quiz](/docs/curriculum/week-4/quiz) -- Scoring, Polish & Publishing
- [Week 5 Quiz](/docs/curriculum/week-5/quiz) -- 3D Space, Camera & Environment
- [Week 6 Quiz](/docs/curriculum/week-6/quiz) -- Interaction, Enemies & Storytelling
- [Week 7 Quiz](/docs/curriculum/week-7/quiz) -- Polish, Levels & Publishing

## Printing

All worksheets are designed to print well on standard letter paper. Use your browser's print function (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) or generate PDFs using the build scripts in the repository.
