# Week 4 Practice Quiz -- Scoring, Polish & Publishing

---

### 1. What is a variable in a game?

- A. Something that changes the graphics
- B. A named value the game tracks, like score or lives
- C. A type of enemy
- D. A visual effect

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**B. A named value the game tracks.** Variables store numbers (score = 100) or text (playerName = "Alex") that can change during gameplay.

</details>

---

### 2. What does HUD stand for?

- A. Highly Useful Data
- B. Heads-Up Display
- C. Hidden User Design
- D. High-Up Dashboard

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**B. Heads-Up Display.** It shows important info (score, lives, health) on screen without being part of the game world.

</details>

---

### 3. What is "juice" in game design?

- A. A health power-up
- B. Small polish effects that make a game feel satisfying
- C. The game's difficulty level
- D. A type of bug

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**B. Small polish effects that make a game feel satisfying.** Screen shake, particles, sound layering, squash-and-stretch -- these tiny details make a huge difference.

</details>

---

### 4. What is a particle effect?

- A. A physics simulation
- B. Small animated images spawned in groups, like sparks or dust
- C. A type of collision
- D. A background image

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**B. Small animated images spawned in groups.** Particles are great for dust clouds, sparkles, explosions, and other effects that need lots of small moving pieces.

</details>

---

### 5. What is the "game loop"?

- A. A type of background music
- B. The cycle from title screen to gameplay to win/lose to retry
- C. When the game repeats the same level
- D. A programming error

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**B. The cycle from title screen to gameplay to win/lose to retry.** Every complete game needs this loop so players can start, play, finish, and play again.

</details>

---

### 6. What is the difference between a sound effect and ambient audio?

- A. Sound effects are louder
- B. Sound effects give instant feedback for actions; ambient audio creates atmosphere
- C. Ambient audio plays only on menus
- D. There is no difference

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**B. Sound effects give instant feedback for actions (jump, coin, hit); ambient audio creates atmosphere (wind, rain, machinery hum).** Both are important for a polished game.

</details>

---

### Bonus: Polish Checklist

Before you "ship" your 2D game, check off each item:

- [ ] Title screen with game name
- [ ] At least 1 complete level
- [ ] Score or collectible tracking
- [ ] Lives or health system
- [ ] Game over screen with retry option
- [ ] At least 2 sound effects
- [ ] At least 1 particle effect
- [ ] Win condition (how does the player "beat" the level?)
