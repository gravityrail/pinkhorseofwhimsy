# Week 1 -- Foundations: Welcome to Game Design

**Date:** April 17, 2026
**Arc:** Foundations
**Guest:** Brian Lowe (extended session -- up to 2 hours)

## Featured Games -- A Tour Through the Decades

Brian introduces games from his own career to show how the medium evolved:

- **720deg** (1986, Midway) -- Early arcade: simple rules, pure skill, quarter-eating design.
- **Space Invaders Extreme** (2008, Backbone) -- Classic 2D reimagined with modern game feel.
- **Sonic Generations** (2011, Backbone/Sega) -- 2D and 3D in one game.
- **Five Nights at Freddy's: Security Breach** (2021, Steel Wool) -- Modern 3D, atmosphere, tension.

*"Here's what the designers were trying to make you feel. Here's what was hard about making it."*

## Brian Lowe -- What It's Like to Make Games (20 min)

- What does a game producer actually do?
- His path: SSI to Midway to Backbone to 2K/Firaxis to Steel Wool and beyond.
- 69 shipped games -- what he learned from the ones that worked and the ones that didn't.
- Advice: *"The most important skill in game development is finishing."*

## Learn

- **What is a game?** Rules, goals, challenge, feedback.
- **Deconstructing a game:** mechanics vs. theme vs. aesthetics.
- **GDevelop tour:** scenes, objects, behaviors, events, preview.
- **Where to find help:** built-in tutorials, asset store, community wiki.

### Key Vocabulary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Mechanic** | A rule or system the player interacts with (e.g., jumping, collecting coins) |
| **Theme** | The story, setting, and visual style of a game |
| **Aesthetics** | The emotional experience the game creates for the player |
| **Scene** | A single screen or level in GDevelop |
| **Object** | Anything you place in a scene (player, enemy, platform, background) |
| **Behavior** | Pre-built logic you attach to an object (e.g., "Platform character") |
| **Event** | An if/then rule: "If [condition], then [action]" |
